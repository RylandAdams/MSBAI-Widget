## Tasks (TASKS.md → desk widget)
The widget's Tasks panel reads `## Active` in `TASKS.md`. A task is one line plus indented
sub bullets; the row opens to show them, and every markdown link becomes clickable.

```
- [ ] **Imperative title** - [MSBAI] for Alex · due Tue Sep 22 · from <meeting> (Sep 21)
  - One plain language sentence: what was asked, by whom, why. Expand acronyms.
  - Decisions, deadlines, numbers, open questions from the meeting, 2 to 5 bullets.
  - [Slack: what the thread is](https://msbai.slack.com/archives/...)
  - [Drive: document name](https://docs.google.com/...)
  - [Gmail: subject](https://mail.google.com/mail/u/0/#all/<threadId>)
  - [Transcript: meeting name](https://app.fireflies.ai/view/<id>)
  - Source: transcripts/YYYY-MM-DD-slug.md
```

**"Task out <meeting>"** means: read the transcript, keep only what I was asked to do or committed to,
then search Slack, Drive and Gmail for the threads and documents each task needs and link them.
Only link URLs a tool actually returned. Ticking the box moves the whole entry to `## Done`.

## Writing
- Plain language, acronyms expanded, short bullets, every link pulled out
- GURU in all caps
- No em dashes, en dashes or hyphens used as connectors
